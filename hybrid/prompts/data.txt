N-03 and N-04 lost power for a combined 19 days after icing on the solar panels in week 7; readings for those days were interpolated from N-02 and N-05 using a lapse rate of 6.5 C per 1,000 m, which likely overstates minimum temperatures by 1 to 2 C. The anemometer at N-04 was replaced on day 61; readings before the replacement read low by roughly 12 percent when compared against the portable reference unit, and have been corrected by a factor of 1.136. Precipitation at elevations above 1,000 m includes snow water equivalent measured by heated gauge, which undercounts in high wind; treat those totals as lower bounds. Uptime is computed over 2,184 scheduled hourly reports per station. Recommended actions: add heating elements to the N-03 and N-04 panels (estimated 2,400 per station), relocate N-06 away from the new parking structure that now shelters it from westerly wind, and schedule calibration of all tipping-bucket gauges before the spring melt. Budget impact for the next quarter is estimated at 7,850 including labor, against a remaining allocation of 11,200.
